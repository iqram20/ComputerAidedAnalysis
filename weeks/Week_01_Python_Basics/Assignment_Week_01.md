# Week 1 Assignment — Python Basics for Engineering

## Submission
Submit one `.ipynb` or `.py` file containing your code, output, and short written answers.

## Part A — Berkeley-aligned practice
1. Calculate the area of a triangle from given base and height.
2. Calculate cylinder surface area and volume.
3. Given two coordinate points, calculate slope and Euclidean distance.
4. Calculate a factorial using the Python standard library.
5. Determine whether each year in a supplied list is a leap year.
6. Compute sinh(x) from exponentials and compare with the built-in function.
7. Numerically verify sin²(x)+cos²(x)=1 for at least five angles.
8. Generate and explain one AttributeError and one TypeError.
9. Create a truth table verifying one of De Morgan's laws.
10. Build XOR using AND, OR, and NOT.

## Part B — Engineering problems
11. Calculate hydrostatic pressure at depths 2, 5, and 10 m.
12. Convert a motor speed of 1750 rpm to rad/s.
13. Resolve a 750 N force at 42° into x- and y-components.
14. Calculate Reynolds number for water in a pipe.
15. Calculate electrical energy consumed by a 2.2 kW machine operating for 3.5 h.
16. Calculate mass flow rate from density and volumetric flow rate.
17. Calculate work done by a force at an angle.
18. Compare two sensor readings and report whether they differ by less than 2%.
19. Evaluate one engineering formula of your choice using at least four variables.
20. Write 3–5 sentences explaining why unit consistency matters in engineering computation.

## Challenge
Approximate π using a finite mathematical series and compare the absolute error with Python's stored `math.pi`.
