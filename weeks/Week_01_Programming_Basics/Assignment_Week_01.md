# Week 1 Assignment — MATLAB Basics for Engineering

## Submission

Submit one MATLAB Live Script (`.mlx`) or MATLAB script (`.m`) containing your code, visible outputs, and short written answers.

Use separate Live Editor sections for each major problem.

## Part A — Basic MATLAB Calculations

1. Calculate the area of a triangle from a given base and height.
2. Calculate cylinder surface area and volume.
3. Given two coordinate points, calculate slope and Euclidean distance.
4. Use `factorial` to calculate 8!.
5. Calculate `sinh(x)` using exponentials and compare with MATLAB's `sinh`.
6. Verify `sin(x)^2 + cos(x)^2 = 1` for at least five angles.
7. Use `sqrt`, `log`, `log10`, `exp`, and `abs` in simple examples.
8. Use `disp` for one result and `fprintf` for another.
9. Create variables with engineering units included in the variable names.
10. Explain the purpose of the semicolon in MATLAB.

## Part B — Engineering Calculations

11. Calculate hydrostatic pressure at depths 2, 5, and 10 m.
12. Convert 1750 rpm to rad/s.
13. Resolve a 750 N force at 42° into x- and y-components.
14. Calculate Reynolds number for water in a pipe.
15. Calculate electrical energy consumed by a 2.2 kW machine operating for 3.5 h.
16. Calculate mass flow rate from density and volumetric flow rate.
17. Calculate work done by a force acting at an angle.
18. Compare two sensor readings and determine whether they differ by less than 2%.
19. Evaluate one engineering formula of your choice using at least four variables.
20. Write 3–5 sentences explaining why unit consistency matters in engineering computation.

## Challenge

Approximate π using a finite mathematical series and compare the result with MATLAB's built-in `pi`.
